Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information Beta AI Apps (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, betaaiapps.com, and how we use it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form or email us, we receive what you choose to share: your name, email address, company, details about your project and your message.
  • Basic technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and it does not load fonts or scripts from other companies.

How we use it

We use the details you send only to read and reply to your inquiry, to discuss a potential project with you, and to keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Who processes it

Messages sent through the contact form may be delivered by a form-processing service acting on our behalf, and are stored in our business email. These providers only process the information to deliver it to us.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the duration of the relationship and any period required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email afex@betaaiapps.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, and the contact form validates input and filters automated spam. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive data through the form.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email afex@betaaiapps.com.
